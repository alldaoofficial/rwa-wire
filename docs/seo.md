# Organic search priorities

Initial technical rollout: 18 October 2026. Search visibility and rankings are outcomes to measure, not guarantees from metadata changes.

The English homepage targets RWA news and asset-tokenization research. The `/news/` hub includes published NEWS and RESEARCH across the editorial sections and links to each article's actual category URL. Homepages have descriptive localized titles; the root homepage declares the RWA Wire WebSite identity. Article schema includes the displayed hero image and an About-page author reference. Drafts and consolidation redirects stay out of the sitemap and RSS.

`npm run build` verifies the rendered identity, canonical article links, complete news-hub coverage and exclusion of noncanonical content from discovery feeds.

Next editorial priorities:

- Improve existing tokenized-deposit, Treasury and stock guides with original comparisons, direct sources and useful explanations. Consolidate overlapping articles instead of creating keyword variants.
- Review legacy automatically generated stories for repeated boilerplate, unsupported claims and little added reporting before promoting them as search landing pages. Never mark them source-reviewed without checking the original evidence.
- Earn relevant references through original reporting and useful research; do not buy links or fabricate editorial credentials.

Measure in Google Search Console using the verified domain property: queries, landing pages, impressions, clicks, countries and search appearance. Compare branded searches such as RWA Wire with specific nonbranded topics. Search-tool results are not a Google rank-tracking report. Search Console performance data was not available through the connected apps during this rollout; do not invent positions, volumes or traffic.
