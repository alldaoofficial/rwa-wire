KuCoin has announced five additional Ondo Stocks products for its spot market: METAon, linked to Meta; TSLAon, linked to Tesla; QQQon, linked to the Invesco QQQ fund; SPYon, linked to the SPDR S&P 500 ETF; and MUon, linked to Micron. The exchange's November 19 notice sets two separate USDT trading windows rather than describing an already completed launch.

According to KuCoin, METAON/USDT and TSLAON/USDT are scheduled to open at 10:00 UTC on November 19, preceded by a one-hour call auction. QQQON/USDT, SPYON/USDT and MUON/USDT are scheduled for 11:00 UTC, also following a one-hour auction. Deposits are supported through the SOL-SPL network, while withdrawals are scheduled to begin at 10:00 UTC on November 20.

KuCoin describes the products as securities-backed tokens providing eligible non-U.S. investors with economic exposure to listed shares or ETFs. The announcement does not establish that token holders receive ordinary shareholder rights. Availability is subject to jurisdictional restrictions, and the planned trading windows should not be mistaken for independently confirmed executions.
