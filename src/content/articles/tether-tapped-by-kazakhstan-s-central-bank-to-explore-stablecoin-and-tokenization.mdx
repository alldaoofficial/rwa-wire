Tether announced on 7 October that it had signed an MOU with the National Bank of Kazakhstan and the Alatau City Authority. The parties will study stablecoin issuance models and prepare a concept and pilot proposal for a tenge-pegged stablecoin.

The agreement also covers a tokenization framework and potential use of Tether's Hadron platform in Alatau. Workshops and training are part of the stated program.

## What the agreement does not establish

A proposal to examine a stablecoin is different from creating one. The release does not provide the reserve composition, redemption terms or customer availability of a launched tenge product.

The involvement of Kazakhstan's central bank also does not, by itself, turn a privately issued token into central-bank money. Readers should distinguish the authority participating in the research from the eventual issuer and legal claim. At this stage, the verified development is a joint exploration program with specified workstreams.
