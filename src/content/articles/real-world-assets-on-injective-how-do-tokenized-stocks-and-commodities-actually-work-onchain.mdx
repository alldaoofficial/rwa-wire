## What the product is

Injective describes its RWA perpetual markets as contracts referencing equities, commodities, currencies and indices. Its TradFi index announcement identifies a perpetual market available through Helix. These are derivative products; the existence of a stock reference does not make the contract an ordinary company share.

## Why the label needs unpacking

“RWA” can describe more than one product structure. A token representing a claim on a held asset and a derivative tracking a reference price answer different investor needs. They should not be combined into an undifferentiated count of tokenized ownership.

For a perpetual market, the relevant documentation is the contract's pricing, margin, funding and settlement rules. For a backed token, the relevant documents include custody and the holder's legal claim.

The editorial takeaway is to identify the instrument before interpreting trading figures. Volume in a stock-linked derivative does not establish how many shares have been brought onchain or how many investors hold ownership claims on those shares.
