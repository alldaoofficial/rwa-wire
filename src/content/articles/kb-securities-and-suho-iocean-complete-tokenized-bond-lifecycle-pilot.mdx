South Korea's KB Securities and blockchain infrastructure provider Suho Iocean have completed a pilot covering the lifecycle of simulated tokenized bonds, from issuance and settlement through maturity redemption. The companies reported the exercise on November 17, 2026, according to separate accounts from Yonhap and Newsis.

The project was conducted under a 2026 blockchain application programming interface development program supported by the Ministry of Science and ICT and the Korea Internet and Security Agency. KB Securities supplied the brokerage use case while Suho Iocean provided the technical infrastructure.

Using the compliance-oriented ERC-3643 standard, the pilot linked KB Securities' bond data to simulated blockchain securities. Its delivery-versus-payment design paired securities transfers with payment so that failure of either side would reverse the transaction. Automated checks between the blockchain and the brokerage's internal records reportedly produced a matching rate above 99.9%. The test also automated maturity payments and token destruction.

Newsis reported a simulated small-ticket investment flow using KB reward points. The participants described possible future stablecoin integration, contingent on legislation. No public commercial launch or live stablecoin settlement was announced.
