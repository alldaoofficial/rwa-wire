OKXICE's public notice dated October 30 lists 50 issuers that objected to tokenized trading of their shares on the proposed venue. The October 28 version listed 38. The 12 additions are CrowdStrike, Corning, Micron Technology, IREN, USA Rare Earth, Bloom Energy, Nike, Nvidia, Palo Alto Networks, Costco, Reddit and Hewlett Packard Enterprise.

The objections concern unaffiliated third-party tokenized NMS stocks offered through OKXICE under the SEC's temporary Innovation Exemption. They block the affected instruments from this venue; they do not prohibit issuer-sponsored tokenization or separate arrangements elsewhere. OKXICE describes a permissioned market using stablecoin pairs and one-for-one share backing, but the notice provides no launch date or reasons for the individual objections. The SEC separately cautions that listing a prospective venue does not establish that it satisfies the exemption's conditions.
