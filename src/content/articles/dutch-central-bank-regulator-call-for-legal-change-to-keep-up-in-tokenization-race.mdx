## RWA Wire analysis

The significance of an institutional tokenization announcement depends on what moves from concept to production. The key distinction is between issuing a token and integrating that token into custody, settlement, collateral and investment workflows. RWA Wire tracks those downstream functions because they reveal whether onchain finance is gaining durable utility.

## What would strengthen the signal
