Centrifuge launched tokenized exposure to the Schroder International Selection Fund Emerging Market Bond strategy on Solana on November 4. The deployment is Schroders' first on the network and brings an actively managed emerging-market debt strategy into public-blockchain distribution. The underlying investment capabilities span sovereign, corporate and local-currency debt rather than the cash and short-duration government securities that have dominated onchain fixed income.

Schroders has invested in emerging-market debt for more than 50 years and has more than 40 dedicated professionals in the asset class. Centrifuge is providing the tokenization infrastructure. The announcement says the fund initially launches on Solana, but it does not specify investor eligibility, subscription terms, assets in the tokenized share class or any confirmed collateral or DeFi integrations. Those details remain important for evaluating practical access and liquidity.
