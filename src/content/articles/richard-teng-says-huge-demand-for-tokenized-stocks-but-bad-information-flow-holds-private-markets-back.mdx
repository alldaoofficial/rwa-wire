## The reported remarks

An October 8, 2026 Binance News item, citing Cointelegraph, reported remarks by Binance co-CEO Richard Teng about demand for tokenized stocks and weak information flows in private markets. This is a reported assessment from an industry participant, rather than an independently measured demand figure.

## Access and information are different problems

A transferable token can change how a position is recorded or exchanged. It does not automatically give a buyer reliable financial statements, a current valuation or a clear account of the rights attached to the instrument.

That distinction is especially relevant when discussing private assets. A product may become easier to transfer while the information needed to assess it remains difficult to obtain.

The useful follow-up is evidence about disclosures and actual participation: which issuers publish usable information, which investors qualify, and what trading or redemption activity occurs. Without that evidence, enthusiasm for tokenization should remain attributed commentary rather than a conclusion that a private-market information problem has been solved.
