BSCN reported on 29 September that Avalanche attracted $131.2 million in tokenized-stock inflows over the preceding seven days. Its report also cited Token Terminal figures showing a $252.1 million increase in tokenized-equity market capitalization over the month ending 25 September.

These are dated observations reported by the outlet, not live figures maintained by RWA Wire. The weekly inflow ranking should not be carried forward as a standing claim that Avalanche leads every measure of stock-token adoption.

## Why the metric matters

New token value, the number of holders and trading activity answer different questions. A concentrated position can change a network's value ranking without demonstrating broad investor participation.

The evidence here supports a specific September comparison. It does not identify how much of the reported change came from new issuance, valuation changes or individual holders. Readers comparing networks should retain the observation window and the metric, rather than treating the headline as a permanent league table.
