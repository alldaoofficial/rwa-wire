Jack Henry announced on October 16 that it plans to integrate with Google Cloud Universal Ledger to support tokenized deposits for banks and credit unions. The company describes itself as the first core banking technology provider planning that connection.

Google Cloud's managed distributed ledger supports programmable transfers and round-the-clock settlement capabilities. Jack Henry says the integration is intended to help community and regional institutions explore new payment and settlement services.

The announcement extends a relationship established in 2022. It describes planned integration and potential customer capabilities; it does not identify a completed rollout or a launch date for individual banks.
