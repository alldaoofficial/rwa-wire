Hong Kong's monetary and securities regulators are encouraging financial institutions to connect tokenised bank deposits with investment products as the city expands its digital-asset infrastructure. In a statement dated November 12, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority said it and the Securities and Futures Commission hosted a seminar the previous day attended by approximately 200 industry practitioners.

The discussion followed the upgrade of Project Ensemble's EnsembleTX infrastructure to support round-the-clock interbank settlement of tokenised deposits. Separately, the SFC has approved 24-hour secondary trading of tokenised money market funds. These are distinct capabilities: continuous settlement between participating banks does not mean every investment product is universally available.

The HKMA said it intends to work with the SFC and market participants to broaden the use of tokenised deposits for settling different investment products. SFC executive director Elisa Ng described the next challenge as connecting investment products, digital money and financial infrastructure to generate practical commercial activity. The statement did not announce a universal retail rollout or provide transaction volumes from the seminar.
