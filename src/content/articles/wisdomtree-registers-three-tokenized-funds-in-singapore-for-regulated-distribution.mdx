WisdomTree has registered three existing tokenized investment funds in Singapore as restricted schemes, opening a regulated route for eligible investors to access different asset classes through local intermediaries. The company announced the development on November 17, 2026, alongside a distribution collaboration with Synthesys Network.

The funds are the WisdomTree Treasury Money Market Digital Fund (WTGXX), the WisdomTree 500 Digital Fund (SPXUX), and the WisdomTree Technology and Innovation 100 Digital Fund (TECHX). They respectively provide exposure to short-term US dollar assets, US large-cap equities, and technology and innovation companies. Registration as restricted schemes does not mean the products are available to all retail investors.

Pinetree Securities, a Singapore Capital Markets Services license holder, is the initial distribution intermediary. Its role includes onboarding and investor due diligence for qualifying institutional clients. Synthesys provides connectivity between regulated distributors and tokenized products, seeking to reduce the need for individual integrations with each fund manager.

WisdomTree says more than 80 channels are connected to Synthesys Network. That figure describes potential distribution reach, not confirmed fund subscriptions or unrestricted availability. Each channel must still satisfy its own regulatory permissions and investor eligibility conditions. The announcement therefore marks an expansion of regulated access infrastructure, rather than a new exchange listing or permissionless trading launch.
