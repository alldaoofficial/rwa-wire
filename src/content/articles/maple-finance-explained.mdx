Maple is an onchain asset manager focused on credit and yield strategies. Its infrastructure connects capital providers with lending strategies while using blockchain rails for allocation, accounting and distribution.

## Market role

Maple offers institutional access with onboarding, legal documentation and due diligence, alongside onchain products designed to integrate with decentralized finance. The platform has increasingly described its model as asset management onchain.

## Products and distribution

Its product set includes secured lending strategies and yield-bearing assets such as syrupUSDC and syrupUSDT. These products can be integrated into other onchain applications, extending the distribution of Maple-managed strategies.

## Risk context

Private and digital-asset credit differs materially from government-backed Treasury exposure. Borrower quality, collateral, liquidation mechanics, liquidity and smart-contract implementation can all affect outcomes.
