Eurex Clearing announced on November 2 that it plans to integrate native digital securities into the same framework, rulebook and risk model used for traditional instruments. The change has not yet been presented as a completed production rollout.

Under the plan, qualifying digital instruments, including European Stability Mechanism issuances, would trade in Eurex Repo's GC Pooling segment, clear through Eurex Clearing and become eligible as margin collateral. Eurex also intends to include the instruments in the ECB GC Pooling collateral basket.

The model is designed to let participants use existing clearing relationships and infrastructure rather than establish a separate digital-market setup. Eurex says concentrating traditional and digital instruments in the same cleared environment should avoid splitting liquidity across parallel markets. The announcement does not specify a launch date, transaction volume or confirmed participant adoption, so those remain open implementation questions.
