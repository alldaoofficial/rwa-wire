Tokenized equities surpassed $4 billion in onchain value for the first time on November 11, according to a November 19 Tokenization Briefing by The Block. The publication estimates that the sector's value has grown to about five times its level at the start of 2026, even as the broader cryptocurrency market has weakened.

The report distinguishes new issuance from changes in the market prices of the underlying shares. Over the eleven weeks ending November 13, newly minted tokens contributed $1.12 billion to sector value, while price movements reduced it by $339 million. The Block attributes roughly four-fifths of that issuance to Binance's bStocks products, with NVIDIA, SpaceX and Circle among the leading exposures.

The analysis also highlights access limitations. It estimates that only about 10% of tokenized equity value is currently accessible to US investors, with much of that exposure concentrated in crypto-related companies. A proposed November 23 expansion through OKXICE is presented as an expectation, not an accomplished launch. These figures describe onchain instruments and should not be interpreted as evidence that token holders necessarily possess the same legal rights as directly registered shareholders.
