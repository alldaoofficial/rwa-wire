Jesse Pollak told The Block on 9 October that equities and non-dollar stablecoins were his leading candidates for the next phase of tokenization. He framed local-currency tokens as potential payment infrastructure for regional fintech companies.

In the same interview, Pollak said Coinbase's stock-token service on Base had launched six weeks earlier and was handling approximately $70 million to $100 million in daily trading. Those numbers are attributed to him rather than presented here as independently audited network statistics.

## Forecast versus observation

The interview combines an executive's strategic priorities, self-reported usage and predictions about future growth. Those categories carry different evidential weight.

The local-currency angle also serves a different use case from stock exposure: payments in a customer's domestic currency, rather than access to a foreign equity. Reading the interview through those separate use cases is more informative than treating a proposed tokenization cycle as a single market-wide outcome.
