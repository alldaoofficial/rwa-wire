Binance added five bStocks as eligible collateral assets at 12:00 UTC on October 28: Bending Spoons, GE Vernova, AppLovin, and two GraniteShares leveraged SK Hynix ETFs. The tokens are available in Cross Margin, Portfolio Margin and Portfolio Margin Pro, with corresponding pairs available for margin trading. Binance explicitly says borrowing is not currently supported.

The expansion is limited by product and user eligibility. Regular, VIP 1 and VIP 2 users must complete a suitability questionnaire, and restricted regions remain excluded. Binance classifies bStocks as certificates representing financial instruments, not shares in the referenced companies or funds. The products do not provide direct ownership, are offered under an approved prospectus in Abu Dhabi Global Market and are not available to U.S. persons. The announcement therefore expands exchange collateral options, not general access to the underlying securities.
