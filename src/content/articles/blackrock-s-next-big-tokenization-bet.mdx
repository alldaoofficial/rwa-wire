CoinDesk's report, published on 3 October and updated on 7 October, examines BlackRock-designed strategies offered through Ondo's Intelligent Portfolios. It describes three portfolio approaches focused on income, diversified growth and higher growth.

The product idea is to represent a portfolio exposure through one token, rather than requiring an investor to hold and rebalance each underlying position separately.

## The structural distinction

The report contrasts that approach with software-managed portfolios in which individual tokenized assets remain in the investor's wallet. Both involve a strategy, but the investor holds a different set of legal and operational building blocks.

RWA Wire's reading is that the story concerns product packaging and distribution, not a promise of better investment performance. A convenient portfolio representation does not tell a reader the strategy's fees, allocation risks or redemption terms. Those questions belong to the actual product documents, rather than being answered by the token format.
