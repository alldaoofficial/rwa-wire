## The research question

An IMF publication from June 2026 examines tokenization across assets and financial infrastructure. Its authors include Tobias Adrian, Carlos Cabedo and Tommaso Mancini-Griffoli. The discussion concerns the architecture of tokenized finance, including the role of money used for settlement.

The earlier version of this article attributed a specific statement to an IMF deputy director. We could not substantiate that attribution from the cited material and have removed it.

## Two claims in one transaction

A tokenized security and its payment asset are distinct claims. Making the security transferable on a ledger does not, by itself, answer what the seller receives or which institution stands behind that payment.

The editorial question is therefore concrete: what is being exchanged, who owes each obligation, and when does settlement become final? Treating every digital payment asset as interchangeable obscures that distinction.

This article is a reading of published research. It is not evidence of a new October policy decision or an IMF requirement that all tokenized securities use one settlement model.
