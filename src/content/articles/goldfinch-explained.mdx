Goldfinch is an onchain credit platform whose current positioning includes Goldfinch Prime, a product designed to bring institutional private-credit strategies onto blockchain rails.

## Goldfinch Prime

Goldfinch describes Prime as a way to access a diversified set of private-credit managers through an onchain structure. The underlying economic exposure remains private credit rather than a crypto-native yield source.

## Why the distinction matters

Private-credit funds lend to businesses and other borrowers outside public bond markets. Returns therefore depend on underwriting, borrower performance, manager selection and the legal structure through which investors receive exposure.

## Risk context

Tokenization can change access and administration, but it does not remove the underlying credit or liquidity risks. Investors need to evaluate the specific vehicle and eligibility terms separately from the blockchain technology.
