Polymesh is a blockchain designed specifically around regulated assets and capital-market workflows. Its architecture differs from general-purpose chains by integrating identity, compliance and asset-management functions into the protocol.

## Purpose-built infrastructure

Participants interacting with identity and regulated-asset functions use verified onchain identities. Issuers can configure transfer restrictions and compliance rules while using native functionality for asset creation, servicing and settlement.

## Asset scope

Polymesh describes use cases spanning equity, debt, commodities, real estate and other regulated financial products. The chain therefore acts as infrastructure rather than as the issuer of one particular RWA.

## Trade-offs

A permissioned identity model can support regulatory workflows, but it is structurally different from fully permissionless blockchain environments. The appropriate model depends on the asset, jurisdiction and market participants involved.
