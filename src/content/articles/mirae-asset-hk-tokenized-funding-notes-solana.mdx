Mirae Asset Securities (HK) completed a tokenized funding-note issuance for eligible institutional investors on Solana. The company said the securities are US dollar-denominated six-month floating-rate notes issued in Hong Kong under its US$3 billion Guaranteed Structured Note Programme. The US$3 billion figure is the programme ceiling, not the size of this issuance, which the release did not disclose.

Marketnode, a Singapore-regulated digital market infrastructure operator, acted as authorised distributor. Mirae Asset Securities managed token issuance, payments and lifecycle administration. The company described the transaction as an extension of its existing structured-product business into digital distribution, not as a retail launch.

The notes were offered only through a private placement to eligible professional investors under Hong Kong law. Mirae Asset Securities also stated that the securities were not authorised by or registered with Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission.
