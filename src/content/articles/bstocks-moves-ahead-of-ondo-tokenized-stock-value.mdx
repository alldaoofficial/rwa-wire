Coinfomania reported on October 26 that an ARK Invest repost had highlighted bStocks moving ahead of Ondo. A live RWA.xyz market snapshot reviewed on October 27 independently showed bStocks at the top of its distributed tokenized-stock league table with $1.3 billion of value, compared with $914.1 million for Ondo.

The ranking measures tokenized-stock value, not the number of available products. RWA.xyz counted 99 bStocks entries and 408 Ondo entries in the same table. Its separate bStocks profile reported $1.33 billion of distributed asset value, 1,580,086 holders and $9.37 billion of monthly transfer volume as of October 27.

RWA.xyz describes bStocks as certificates issued by BTech Holdings Limited, a Binance group affiliate, representing interests in underlying securities held by the issuer. The snapshot documents a current ranking change; it does not establish that bStocks will retain the lead or that the products confer direct ownership of underlying company shares.
