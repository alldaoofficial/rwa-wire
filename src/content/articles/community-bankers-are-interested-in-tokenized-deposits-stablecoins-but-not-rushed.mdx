ABA Banking Journal reported the CSBS community-bank survey results on 6 October. Eighteen percent of respondents said they planned to offer tokenized deposits within the next year, and 16% said the same about stablecoin solutions.

Roughly eight in ten said they neither offered these services nor planned to introduce them over that period. Current reported use was much smaller: approximately 4% for tokenized deposits and 3% for stablecoin solutions, with some existing providers planning to exit.

## Reading the survey correctly

These answers describe bankers' stated intentions and current offerings. They do not measure the value of customer deposits moved through a network, nor establish that announced plans will be delivered.

The useful finding is the gap between interest and implementation. Reporting only the planned-adoption percentages would miss both the large group with no near-term plans and the respondents reconsidering services they already offer.
