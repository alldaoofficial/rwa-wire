TokenPost reported on October 21 that Spiko's assets under management had crossed $3 billion after increasing 14.07% over 30 days. The report put the monthly increase at about $374 million and said the move from $2 billion to $3 billion took less than four months.

The milestone follows Spiko's October 6 company update, which reported $2.7 billion placed in its funds and growth of more than fivefold over 12 months. That primary release also said more than 10,000 businesses and individuals in over 25 countries used the platform. Spiko offers funds denominated in euros, dollars, pounds sterling and Swiss francs.

The figures describe aggregate assets across Spiko's fund range, not a token price or a single blockchain product. Currency conversion, subscriptions, redemptions and differences in product coverage can affect comparisons with third-party onchain rankings. The accessible sources do not document a reproducible basis for the separate headline claim of exactly 536% annual growth, so that figure is excluded.
