Allfunds and the Solana Foundation named the first cohort of Project Harmonia on November 16, 2026, moving their tokenized-fund distribution initiative from recruitment toward planned product launches. The group comprises 22 asset managers, including Apollo Global Management, Fidelity International and Schroders, according to reports from Blockstories and Solana Compass.

The program is designed to connect Solana-issued investment funds with Allfunds' established distribution network. Infrastructure provider ioBuilders supplies the Asseto integration layer, while Particula supports fund assessment and admission. Blockstories reports that roughly 40 managers applied and 22 were accepted, with checks covering ownership structures, legal domicile and onchain implementation.

The first products are expected to become available through Allfunds in the first quarter of 2027. Additional funds still being developed are planned for the first half of that year. These are forward-looking launch targets rather than evidence that the cohort's funds are already commercially available. The critical test will be whether institutional distributors adopt the products and generate sustained investment flows.
