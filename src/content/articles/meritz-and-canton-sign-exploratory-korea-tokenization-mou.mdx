Meritz Securities and the Canton Foundation announced a strategic memorandum of understanding on October 14. The parties plan to evaluate tokenization of Korean financial assets and whether Canton's privacy-enabled infrastructure could support institutional activity in the country's capital markets. The agreement is exploratory: it does not launch a tokenized security, trading venue or production service.

The proposed cross-border work could involve global institutional partners and sovereign-grade debt assets. The parties also intend to study whether tokenized high-quality liquid assets could move between execution and clearing environments or serve as institutional collateral. Any expansion is expected to proceed in stages and remain subject to South Korea's developing digital-asset rules. The announcement provides no implementation timetable or named asset for issuance.
