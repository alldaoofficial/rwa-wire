Maybank has completed a tokenized bond settlement pilot with Etiqa Life Insurance Berhad, its insurance arm, according to a November 20 announcement from technology provider SettleMint. The transaction exchanged bonds represented in tokenized form against tokenized bank deposits on Maybank's permissioned blockchain network.

SettleMint said its Digital Asset Lifecycle Platform supported the pilot. Maybank's Rivai Tan described the arrangement as a delivery-versus-payment workflow combining tokenized securities, deposits and custody processes across blockchain and conventional infrastructure. This is a controlled test, not an announcement of a broadly available bond trading or settlement service.

The bank has also been testing tokenized deposits and near real-time cross-border payments in a wider program, the release said. The transaction with a related insurance company provides a concrete demonstration of the proposed settlement mechanics, while leaving questions about third-party participation, interoperability and commercial scale unresolved. SettleMint said Maybank is assessing its next phase rather than announcing a launch timetable.
