Wirex announced on November 17 that tokenized exposure to US stocks is available through its infrastructure platform and the Wirex One application. The company says eligible customers can buy and hold blockchain-based tokens referencing shares such as Apple, Amazon and Microsoft, with around-the-clock access rather than conventional exchange trading hours.

According to the company's release, the tokens operate on the Base network and are issued by Coinbase. Wirex describes them as backed one-for-one by shares, although that claim is made by the issuer's distribution partner and does not itself establish independent reserve verification. Wirex also offers the product to businesses through its existing infrastructure API and software development kit, allowing partners to integrate access into their own applications.

Wirex says its Wirex One app is the first deployment of the offering and refers to nearly two million users. That figure describes the app's stated audience, not confirmed tokenized-stock holders or trading activity. Availability depends on jurisdiction and eligibility.

Crucially, the company's disclaimer says these products do not transfer legal ownership of the underlying shares or shareholder rights, including voting. The announcement therefore marks a new distribution option for tokenized market exposure, not a replacement for directly holding listed equities.
