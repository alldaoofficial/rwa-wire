Ondo Finance is a project focused on issuing tokenized exposure to traditional fixed-income instruments, most prominently short-duration U.S. Treasuries, aimed at both institutional and onchain participants seeking yield-bearing, dollar-denominated tokens.

## What it offers

Ondo's products are structured similarly to money market or short-duration bond funds: investor funds are used to purchase underlying Treasuries or related instruments, and investors receive tokens representing their proportional share of that fund. As the underlying assets earn interest, that yield is reflected in the token's value or distributed to holders, depending on the specific product's structure.

## Positioning in the RWA landscape

The project is among a group of issuers — alongside several traditional asset managers now running their own tokenization programs — that have contributed to tokenized Treasuries becoming the largest category of real-world assets onchain by total value.

## What to check before evaluating any product

Details like minimum investment size, redemption timelines, eligible investor categories, and which blockchains a product is issued on vary between Ondo's offerings and change over time. Reviewing the issuer's own current offering documents is necessary before forming any view on a specific product — this profile is descriptive, not a recommendation.
