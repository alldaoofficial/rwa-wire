OKXICE updated its public notice on October 26 to add IBM, IonQ, Oracle and Intel to the issuers objecting to tokenized trading of their shares on the planned venue. The primary notice now names 26 objecting issuers, up from 22 in the October 23 version. The four additions are a new expansion of the venue-specific objection list, not a restatement of the earlier disclosures.

The objections concern tokenized NMS stocks created by an unaffiliated third party and proposed for trading through OKXICE under the SEC's temporary Innovation Exemption. The SEC says issuers must be able to object and prevent their securities from trading on a tokenized securities venue. Accordingly, the notices stop these third-party instruments from trading on OKXICE under the exemption; they do not prohibit issuer-sponsored tokenization or arrangements on other venues.

OKXICE remains a planned, permissioned venue rather than a confirmed live market. Its October 26 notice describes Uniswap v4 pools on XLayer, participant identity and sanctions screening, and one-for-one backing for unaffiliated third-party tokens. The notice provides no reason for the four companies' decisions and no launch date.
