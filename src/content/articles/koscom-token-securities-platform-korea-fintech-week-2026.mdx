Koscom plans to present its shared token-securities platform at Korea Fintech Week 2026, scheduled for November 25–27 at the aT Center in Seoul. The October 13 report describes an upcoming showcase, not the launch of a live commercial service.

## Shared market infrastructure

The platform is intended to support issuance, account management and connections to related systems. Koscom says the design can link brokerages' existing ledgers with blockchain-based distributed ledgers. The company also says 13 securities firms have signed cooperation agreements for token-securities business.

Koscom's earlier primary documentation describes the project as common infrastructure that multiple securities firms can use to reduce duplicate system investment. It also records completed testing with Korea Securities Depository for aggregate-supply management and an issuance-infrastructure collaboration with LG CNS. Those milestones establish technical preparation, but they do not by themselves establish production availability or regulatory approval for token-securities offerings.
