Franklin Templeton's sgBENJI tokenized money-market fund share count on XRP Ledger increased substantially on October 22. The ledger explorer Bithomp records 192,698,409.6219078 shares outstanding, one active holder and a last update at 04:13 UTC. CoinDesk reported that two batches of 72,184,793 shares were added at 02:58 and 04:13 UTC. At the recorded $1.039 share price, those additions represent about $150 million and bring the displayed supply value close to $200 million.

Franklin Templeton's Singapore product sheet says the token represents shares in the Franklin OnChain U.S. Dollar Short-Term Money Market Fund. The fund invests in high-quality U.S.-dollar debt and money-market instruments, while its register combines blockchain records with possible off-chain information. The document names Stellar and XRP Ledger as supported public networks.

The ledger shows the entire supply in one address. That concentration does not prove a single beneficial owner, and the available sources do not identify the investor or investors behind the address. The event therefore documents a large issuance on XRP Ledger, not broad distribution or secondary-market adoption.
