OKXICE's public notice dated October 28 lists 38 issuers that have objected to inclusion on its proposed tokenized-securities venue. The five latest additions are Walmart, Eli Lilly, Carnival, Arcos Dorados and Cigna, raising the disclosed total from 33 in the previous update. The notice records objections but does not give each issuer's reasons.

OKXICE describes the planned venue as a joint venture between Intercontinental Exchange and OKC USA. Its design would place tokenized U.S. securities in permissioned Uniswap v4 liquidity pools on XLayer, paired with stablecoins. The notice also says the platform is not SEC-registered for activities conducted under the cited exemption. The latest additions therefore represent issuer opt-outs from a proposed market-structure pilot, not a rejection of tokenization in every form.
