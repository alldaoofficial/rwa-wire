Binance Research's September report says the tokenized-stock category exceeded $3 billion in market capitalization, citing RWA.xyz data as of 28 September. This is a historical snapshot, not a live market total.

The original headline used that milestone to assert that the onchain economy was no longer speculative. The underlying number supports a narrower conclusion: a defined category had reached a reported size on a particular date.

## Reading the number

Market capitalization describes outstanding value at a snapshot. Trading volume counts transactions over a period; transfer volume counts movements between addresses. The same asset can move repeatedly, so those measures are not interchangeable.

The report is also produced by an exchange participating in this market. Attribution and the observation date belong alongside its figures. RWA Wire's interpretation is that the milestone is useful for tracking category size, while claims about liquidity or investor adoption require separate evidence.
