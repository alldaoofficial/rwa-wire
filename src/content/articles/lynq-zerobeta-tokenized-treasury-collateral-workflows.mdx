Lynq and ZeroBeta announced on October 27 that ZeroBeta's BlueShift risk and collateral platform has certified connectivity to Lynq's settlement and collateral mobility network. The companies say the integration lets institutions use eligible tokenized Treasury holdings for secured borrowing or digital-asset trading margin while the underlying asset continues accruing interest.

BlueShift sets collateral terms, monitors margin and sends pledge, top-up or release instructions. Lynq holds the collateral and executes those instructions on its Avalanche-based settlement rail. For lending, BlueShift can adjust pledged collateral as its value moves against an agreed threshold. For trading, it calculates margin for positions and orders before directing Lynq to pledge or release the asset.

The announcement describes an operational connection, not demonstrated market usage. It names no lender, exchange or trading desk that has completed a transaction through the integration and discloses no collateral amount, borrowing rate, haircut or margin threshold. Lynq separately reports more than $89 million in platform assets and over 40 institutional firms onboarded, but those network-wide figures do not measure adoption of the ZeroBeta workflow.
