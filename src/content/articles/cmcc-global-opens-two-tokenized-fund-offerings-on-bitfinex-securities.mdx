CMCC Global announced on October 22 that subscriptions had opened for tokenized interests in two existing multi-manager funds: the U.S.-dollar-denominated Crest Fund and the Bitcoin-denominated Liberty Bitcoin Accumulation Fund. Tether's Hadron platform provides the tokenization infrastructure, while Bitfinex Securities handles the offering and planned secondary trading under its El Salvador framework.

The subscription period runs through October 30, 2026, for eligible investors. CMCC said each token represents the relevant participating fund interest on a one-for-one economic basis and remains subject to KYC and AML controls. Crest tokens are offered at US$10, while LBAF tokens are priced at 0.0001 BTC.

BTCS S.A. has agreed to act as an anchor investor and intends to allocate an undisclosed triple-digit amount of Bitcoin to LBAF. That wording describes an intended allocation, not a completed transfer. CMCC says Crest targets returns above U.S. Treasury-bill yields, while LBAF targets net annualized returns of 8% to 12% measured in Bitcoin; these are investment objectives, not guaranteed outcomes.
