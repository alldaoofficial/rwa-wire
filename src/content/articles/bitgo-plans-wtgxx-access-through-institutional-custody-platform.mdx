BitGo Bank & Trust announced on October 27 that it plans to integrate the WisdomTree Treasury Money Market Digital Fund, WTGXX, into BitGo Mint for eligible institutional clients. The companies describe a future integration rather than a service that is already live.

Once available, eligible clients are expected to submit purchase and redemption instructions through BitGo Bank & Trust and move between U.S. dollars, supported stablecoins and fund shares without separately onboarding with WisdomTree. BitGo also plans to support peer-to-peer reallocation of beneficial interests between eligible clients through its Go Network settlement system.

The announcement includes important limits. Launch timing remains under development and may change. Collateral functionality, peer-to-peer transfers and certain stablecoin services may not be available at launch. BitGo Bank & Trust will provide shareholder servicing and sub-accounting for WTGXX, but is not the fund's investment adviser, broker-dealer or distributor. Fund shares are investments rather than bank deposits and are not FDIC-insured.
