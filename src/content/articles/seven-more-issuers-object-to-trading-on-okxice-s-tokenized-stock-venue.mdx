OKXICE updated its public notice on October 19 to identify seven additional issuers that objected to tokenized trading of their shares on its proposed venue. Goldman Sachs, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Caterpillar, McDonald's, Western Digital and Alphabet joined the nine issuers disclosed in the previous notice, taking the total to 16. Alphabet's two listed share classes count as one issuer.

The objections apply specifically to OKXICE's use of unaffiliated third-party tokenization under the SEC's temporary exemption. A timely objection prevents that venue from making the affected tokenized stock available. It does not prohibit issuer-sponsored tokenization or another platform operating under a separate legal arrangement. The update materially reduces the recognizable large-cap names available from OKXICE's original proposed roster, but it is not evidence that the venue has launched or begun trading.
