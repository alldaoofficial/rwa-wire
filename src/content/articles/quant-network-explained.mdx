Quant Network is an infrastructure project centered on interoperability — the ability for different blockchains, and for blockchains and traditional financial networks, to communicate and transact with one another.

## What it builds

The project's core technology, Overledger, is designed as a connective layer that lets applications interact with multiple distinct blockchain networks without needing to build separate integrations for each one. The stated aim is to reduce fragmentation as financial infrastructure providers adopt different, often incompatible, blockchain platforms for tokenization and settlement projects.

## Where it fits in the tokenization landscape

As more banks, asset managers and market infrastructure providers run tokenization pilots on different underlying networks, interoperability has become a practical bottleneck: an asset tokenized on one network is not automatically usable on another. Quant has positioned itself around this problem, working with financial standards bodies and infrastructure providers on interoperability specifications.
