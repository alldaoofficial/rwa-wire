## What happened

Reuters reported on October 5, 2026 that the OKX–ICE joint venture filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission to launch a tokenized trading platform. The proposed service would support round-the-clock trading.

## A filing is not a launch

The distinction matters because a proposed venue, a regulatory exemption and a customer-facing product are separate milestones. This report establishes that an application was made; it does not establish that the service was already operating or open to every investor.

A September SEC order provides a useful policy reference for certain tokenized-securities venues. Its conditions and scope should be read directly rather than generalized into permission for all blockchain stock trading.

The practical follow-up is the venture's actual authorization, operating rules and launch notice. Readers will also need to know which instruments and participants qualify. Until those details are confirmed, a 24-hour trading ambition remains a product proposal rather than a demonstrated change in market access.
