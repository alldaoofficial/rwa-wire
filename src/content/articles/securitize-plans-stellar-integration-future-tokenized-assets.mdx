Securitize announced on October 28 that it plans to integrate the Stellar network into its tokenization infrastructure. The proposed connection would let Securitize support the issuance and ongoing management of tokenized financial assets on Stellar for institutional asset managers. The announcement accompanied Meridian 2026 in Lisbon.

This is a planned integration, not a completed deployment or product launch. Securitize and the Stellar Development Foundation did not identify a first asset, participating issuer, launch date or live contract. The release says details about supported assets will be announced later and warns that the integration could be delayed, changed or not completed.

Securitize described the plan as an extension of its multichain approach. The company reported approximately $5 billion in tokenized securities on its platform as of September 30, 2026, but that platform-wide figure does not represent assets already issued on Stellar. The practical impact will depend on which managers and products use the network after integration is completed.
