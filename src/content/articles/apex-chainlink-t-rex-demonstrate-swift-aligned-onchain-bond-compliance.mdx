Apex Group, Chainlink, T-REX Network and the ERC-3643 Association demonstrated a modular compliance framework for onchain bonds aligned with Swift standards. The initial implementation represents a bond through T-REX infrastructure, applies ERC-3643 identity, investor-eligibility and transfer controls, and uses Swift taxonomy to keep the asset representation consistent with established market practices.

Chainlink contributes CCIP for cross-chain token transfers, its Automated Compliance Engine for policy enforcement and Data Streams for pricing. T-REX provides deployment and a single source of record across supported blockchain networks, while Apex supplies the regulated asset-servicing environment. The design is intended to let institutions define compliance requirements once and reuse them across networks.

Swift has also joined the ERC-3643 Association. The announcement presents the work as an implementation demonstration connected to the Singapore FinTech Festival; it does not identify a new bond issuer, issuance amount, capital raise or production launch.
