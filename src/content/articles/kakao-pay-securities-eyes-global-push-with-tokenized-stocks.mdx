Kakaopay Securities is studying how Korean-listed equities could reach overseas investors through tokenization. Ondo's 29 September announcement describes an MOU covering distribution infrastructure, joint research and a task force for overseas retail opportunities.

Dinari's announcement on the same date describes a separate collaboration: sourcing the underlying shares, testing tokenization through a proof of concept and evaluating operational, technical and regulatory requirements.

## Two complementary workstreams

The Ondo agreement focuses on a potential distribution network. The Dinari agreement focuses on demonstrating the proposed mechanism. Neither announcement supplies final customer terms or establishes that international investors can already buy these products.

The distinction keeps the story grounded in what has happened: partnerships have been agreed and evaluation work has been outlined. Turning that work into a service requires a defined instrument and a permitted route to the intended investors; a successful technical demonstration alone would not settle those questions.
