Clearstream said on October 28 that Luxembourg's Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier authorized it to act as a Control Agent under Blockchain Law IV. The role lets Clearstream oversee tokenized issuance by maintaining the central issuance account on a distributed ledger, tracking holdings across account keepers and reconciling issued volume with recorded positions.

The planned service is designed for selected share classes within existing UCITS or alternative-investment-fund umbrella structures. Transfer agents, fund administrators, custodians and depositaries can retain their current roles while Clearstream supplies the DLT issuance and control layer.

Clearstream describes a permissioned environment supporting subscriptions, transfers and redemptions after validated instructions, with whitelisting intended to restrict holdings to authorized securities accounts. The authorization is a regulatory milestone, not evidence that the full platform is already operational: Clearstream states that its integrated Control Agent service remains subject to completion of CSSF regulatory processes.
