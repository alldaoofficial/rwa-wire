Vantage Bank and Rio Bank completed a principal-and-interest payment for a loan participation using tokenized deposits on Hazel Network, according to loan-sales platform Participate. Vantage posted the payment in Participate, which allocated principal and interest before transmitting the funds to the buying institution's digital wallet. Participate said the process took less than three minutes and updated the related loan balances and payment notices as the funds moved.

The transaction used Hazel's bank and blockchain infrastructure; it did not tokenize ownership of the underlying loan. Participate coordinated allocations, instructions and servicing records, while Vantage supplied banking infrastructure and Custodia supplied wallets, compliance functions and blockchain operations. A June technical announcement said Hazel's reference implementation had been live on Ethereum mainnet since March 2026. Participate characterized the October transaction as the first completed payment under a collaboration announced in April.
