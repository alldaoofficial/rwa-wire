The Eurosystem invited market participants on November 20, 2026 to join analytical work and practical experimentation under its Appia initiative, which is intended to help design an integrated European tokenized financial ecosystem.

The invitation identifies three areas for collaboration: tokenized deposits and related payments, tokenized securities, and governance arrangements for shared networks. Participation is organized through collective initiatives involving independent legal entities, rather than individual applications. Applicants must identify the design streams they intend to address and demonstrate relevant expertise and capacity to contribute.

The deadline for applications to the first experimentation exercises is December 11, 2026 at 12:00 CET. Submissions received later will be considered on a rolling basis. The Eurosystem says it will assess proposals for relevance, practical experience, technical readiness, tangible outputs and resource commitments, while also considering geographic and stakeholder diversity.

This is an invitation to collaborate on research and tests, not an announcement that a new tokenized-deposit or securities platform has entered commercial operation.
