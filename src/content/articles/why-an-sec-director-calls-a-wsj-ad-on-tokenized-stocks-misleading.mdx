In an Unchained interview excerpt published on November 18, Jamie Selway, director of the US Securities and Exchange Commission's Division of Trading and Markets, challenged an advertisement warning public-company executives about tokenized shares. The advertisement appeared in the Wall Street Journal in late October and was attributed in the interview to the Common Sense Leadership Fund.

Selway said the advertisement was misleading in two respects. First, he disputed the suggestion that the relevant tokenized-share trading arrangements were entirely unregulated, pointing to requirements associated with exempt entities, including permissioned wallets, public notice and SEC examinations. Second, he rejected the implication that a token necessarily separates holders from the rights attached to the underlying security. His comments described the framework under discussion; they did not announce a new regulation.

Brett Redfearn, president of Securitize, said his company had run a responding advertisement and argued that incumbent intermediaries may view alternative trading structures as competitive threats. The conversation also discussed issuer objections to the OKX ICE tokenized-stock initiative. Those objections and the October advertisement provide context for the November interview, rather than evidence of a newly launched trading product.
