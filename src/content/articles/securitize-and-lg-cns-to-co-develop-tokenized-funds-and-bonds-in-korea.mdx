Securitize and LG CNS announced a memorandum of understanding on 6 October to explore tokenized assets and digital-asset infrastructure for South Korean financial institutions.

The companies intend to combine Securitize's securities infrastructure with LG CNS's technology capabilities and local financial-sector relationships. The stated scope includes institutional use cases, tokenization solutions and stablecoin-related infrastructure.

## Partnership versus product

The original announcement describes areas of cooperation, not the terms of an investable fund or bond. It states that contemplated products remain subject to applicable law, regulatory approvals and the companies' own compliance processes.

For readers following Korea's market, the concrete development is therefore a cross-border infrastructure partnership. A subsequent product announcement would need to identify the issuer, investor eligibility and the actual instrument. Those details are absent from this memorandum, so the agreement should not be presented as proof of live tokenized-fund distribution.
