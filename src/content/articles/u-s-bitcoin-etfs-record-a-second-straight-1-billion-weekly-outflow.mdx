U.S. spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded another week of heavy net withdrawals during November 9–13, 2026. The Block reported approximately $1.07 billion in weekly outflows, citing its analysis of SoSoValue figures, after roughly $1.28 billion had left the funds in the preceding week. That marked two consecutive weeks with more than $1 billion in redemptions.

A separate daily fund-flow table maintained by Farside Investors shows net outflows on all five trading days of November 9–13. Its figures include approximately $201 million on Monday, $95 million on Tuesday, $300 million on Wednesday, $284 million on Thursday and $185 million on Friday. The final session's losses included about $143.5 million from BlackRock's IBIT and $41.9 million from Fidelity's FBTC.

These figures describe flows into and out of listed investment products, rather than Bitcoin's total trading volume. They also come from two data compilations with slightly different rounding. The sustained withdrawals offer a useful measure of institutional-market sentiment, but do not by themselves establish what caused the redemptions or predict subsequent prices.
