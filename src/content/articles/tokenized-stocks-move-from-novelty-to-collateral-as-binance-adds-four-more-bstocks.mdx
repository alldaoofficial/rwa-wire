## Four additional instruments

Binance's October 7, 2026 announcement added four more bStocks to its supported collateral assets. The instruments reference JPMorgan Chase, Eli Lilly, Securitize and StablecoinX. Our original article was published on October 9; that publication date should not be confused with the announcement date.

## What collateral support changes

A collateral listing can make an asset useful for more than outright trading. However, eligibility is only the starting point. Valuation discounts, position limits and liquidation arrangements determine how much borrowing capacity an instrument actually supplies.

A holder also needs to understand the underlying claim. A token linked to a company's stock price should not be assumed to provide the same rights as a share registered directly with the company.

For this development, the next useful evidence would be collateral balances and borrowing activity for the newly supported instruments. Without those figures, describing the expansion as demonstrated institutional adoption would go beyond the announcement.
