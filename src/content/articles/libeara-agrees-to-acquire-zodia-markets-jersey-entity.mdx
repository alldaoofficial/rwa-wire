Libeara has entered a definitive agreement to acquire Zodia Markets (Jersey) Limited from the Zodia Markets group. The companies did not disclose the transaction's financial terms, and completion remains subject to regulatory approval. The announcement is therefore an agreed acquisition, not a completed transfer.

The target is regulated by the Jersey Financial Services Commission for investment business and is supervised as a virtual asset service provider, according to Libeara. After completion, Libeara plans to rename it Libeara (Jersey) Limited.

Libeara already holds a Capital Markets Services licence from the Monetary Authority of Singapore. The Jersey acquisition would add a regulated European operating base to a group that says its infrastructure has supported more than US$1 billion of regulated tokenized assets. The official release did not announce a live intraday fund-dealing service.
