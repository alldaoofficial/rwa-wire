OKXICE updated its public notice on October 27 to identify 33 issuers that objected to tokenized trading of their shares on the planned venue. The previous notice, dated October 26, listed 26. The seven additions are JPMorgan Chase, Advanced Micro Devices, Applied Materials, Coca-Cola, Adobe, Dell Technologies and Sandisk. This is a new expansion of the venue-specific list rather than a recap of earlier objections.

The objections concern unaffiliated third-party tokenized versions of the companies' stocks on OKXICE. They do not amount to general prohibitions on issuer-sponsored tokenization or activity on other venues. The public notice does not state why the seven companies objected.

OKXICE is still a prospective venue. The SEC's Innovation Exemption page says a tokenized securities venue must publish its notice at least 30 calendar days before operating. It also cautions that listing OKXICE as a notifier does not establish that the venue satisfies the exemption's conditions or that the SEC has validated its notice.
