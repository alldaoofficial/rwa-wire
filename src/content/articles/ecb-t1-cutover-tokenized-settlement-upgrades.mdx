ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone said Europe will shorten its standard securities settlement cycle from T+2 to T+1 on October 11, 2027. The Eurosystem plans changes to TARGET2-Securities night-time settlement processes in June 2027, ahead of the cutover.

Cipollone presented the timetable alongside the Eurosystem’s tokenization work. Pontes has settled wholesale tokenized-asset transactions in central bank money since September 21, 2026. The ECB plans to extend its operating hours significantly by mid-2027, offer immediate finality on the Eurosystem DLT platform as early as 2027, and target continuous availability in 2028.

The speech did not describe tokenized platforms as replacing existing infrastructure. Cipollone said T2S will continue operating while new platforms develop, with interoperability needed to prevent new digital silos. Appia is expected to produce a blueprint for an integrated European tokenized-finance ecosystem by 2028.
