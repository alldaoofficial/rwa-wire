Ondo announced its Tangem integration on November 25, extending distribution beyond conventional trading interfaces. The issuer says the supported networks are Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana. Its portfolio products package investment strategies into individual tokens, rather than requiring users to assemble each position separately.

BSCN reports that the assets appear in Tangem's real-assets section and that redemption remains a separate process handled by Ondo. The wallet provider does not issue the securities products. Regional eligibility restrictions continue to apply, and keeping private keys offline does not remove liquidity or issuer risk. The announcement therefore represents an expansion of access infrastructure, not unrestricted access to conventional brokerage accounts.
