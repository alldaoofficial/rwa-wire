Fission announced on October 15 that it closed a $10 million Series A led by Pantera Capital. Participants included Circle Ventures, New Form, gumi Cryptos Capital, Borderless Capital and GTS chief executive Ari Rubenstein. The company provides liquidity infrastructure for supported tokenized fund products, allowing eligible holders to access capital without waiting for conventional fund redemption cycles. Fission says its platform is designed for institutional and permissioned markets, including investor-eligibility, transfer-restriction and compliance controls. The new funding is intended to expand liquidity capacity, supported asset coverage and institutional integrations. Fission also reported processing millions of dollars in transactions, but did not disclose an exact amount, valuation or expansion timetable.
