## What to watch

## The market-structure angle

The important question is what the tokenized instrument can actually do after issuance. Collateral eligibility, transfer restrictions, custody, redemption and settlement determine whether a digital representation becomes useful financial infrastructure rather than simply a new wrapper around an existing asset.

## What would confirm adoption

Watch for recurring use, additional eligible assets, institutional counterparties and integration with trading or lending workflows. Expansion across those dimensions would be stronger evidence of adoption than token issuance by itself.
