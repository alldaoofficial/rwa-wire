Ant International disclosed live Hong Kong-dollar interbank transfers completed during September and October 2026 with Bank of China (Hong Kong), China Construction Bank (Asia) and HSBC. Ant's WhaleRTP wholesale settlement platform initiated the payments using tokenized deposits, while the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's EnsembleTX infrastructure settled the interbank leg in tokenized central-bank money. Ant said each transfer moved from payment instruction to final receipt within five minutes.

The transactions form a concrete corporate-treasury use case for moving liquidity across banks outside conventional operating windows. HKMA separately confirmed that EnsembleTX now supports 24/7 settlement and has expanded to 12 participating banks and 20 industry pioneers. Ant says the setup reduced reliance on prefunding, but it did not publish transaction values or an independently measured capital reduction. Its proposed move toward AI-directed agentic treasury remains a future plan, not part of the completed transfers.
