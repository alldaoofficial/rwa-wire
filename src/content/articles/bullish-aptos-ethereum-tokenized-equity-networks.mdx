Bullish named Solana, Aptos and Ethereum as the initial network partners for its issuer-sponsored equity tokenization service on November 3. Solana is the existing foundation; Bullish said it plans to extend the same capabilities to Aptos and Ethereum. The announcement is therefore a network roadmap, not evidence that tokenized shares are already live on the two additional chains.

The decision follows an October 27 demonstration at the New York Stock Exchange. Bullish and transfer agent Equiniti moved 61 million reserved Bullish shares on Solana, with Equiniti administering the transfer. Bullish says its multi-network model would let issuers reach investors across several public blockchains while the transfer agent retains the official ownership record.

That structure is important for regulated equities because the onchain token is intended to remain connected to the issuer's shareholder register. The release does not identify the first third-party issuer for Aptos or Ethereum or give a launch date for either integration.
