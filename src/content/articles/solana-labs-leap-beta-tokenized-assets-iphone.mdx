Leap, a company under Solana Labs, opened an early-access beta of its mobile trading app on November 5. The iPhone app is distributed through Apple's public TestFlight program and is not available in the United Kingdom or other restricted regions. An Android version is planned but has not launched.

The app brings tokenized stocks, exchange-traded funds, metals, currencies and crypto into one interface on Solana. It also pairs price charts with curated news and tracks profit and loss across account activity. Trades execute as onchain swaps. Leap charges no platform fee at launch, although users still face network and routing costs. The company says it may introduce fees later; that remains a plan rather than a current charge. The beta status and geographic limits mean this is an early product release, not unrestricted general availability.
