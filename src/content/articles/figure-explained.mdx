Figure is a blockchain-native capital marketplace focused heavily on credit. Its model connects origination, funding, sale and trading while recording and administering parts of those workflows on blockchain infrastructure.

## Capital-market stack

Figure Connect serves as a marketplace connecting loan originators with capital providers. Democratized Prime extends the stack into an onchain lend-borrow marketplace, while DART provides digital asset registry infrastructure.

## Tokenized money

The ecosystem also includes $YLDS, which Figure describes as an SEC-registered yield-bearing stablecoin operating as a tokenized money-market fund.

## Why it matters for RWA

Figure illustrates a broader definition of tokenization: moving the lifecycle of loans and credit-market assets onto blockchain rails, rather than simply wrapping a conventional security in a token.
