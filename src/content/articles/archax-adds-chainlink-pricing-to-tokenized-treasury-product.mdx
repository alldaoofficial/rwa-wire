Archax said on October 19 that it adopted Chainlink Data Streams to supply current pricing inputs for $GOVY, its auto-rolling tokenized U.S. Treasury product. $GOVY gives eligible investors legally enforceable exposure to short-dated Treasury bills held one-to-one in regulated custody. Northern Trust holds the underlying securities, and Archax says each maturing bill is replaced with an equivalent short-dated security.

The pricing integration is intended to support valuation in onchain lending, collateral, margin and treasury-management workflows. Archax also lists transfers between eligible whitelisted wallets and subscription or redemption using supported stablecoins. The announcement describes capabilities and prospective applications; it does not identify a live lender, an assigned collateral haircut or a launched $GOVY credit market.
