The Block reported on 10 October that U.S. spot ether ETFs lost $542.1 million over the preceding week, while Solana funds lost $24.8 million. Its analysis of SoSoValue data put ether's outflow streak at nine trading days and said Solana's 14-week inflow run had ended.

These are fund-flow figures for the named crypto ETF categories. They should not be presented as redemptions from tokenized money-market funds or tokenized stocks.

## Why this belongs in market coverage

The figures describe one channel through which investors adjust crypto exposure. They provide a dated market observation, rather than evidence about the demand for every product using blockchain infrastructure.

RWA Wire does not infer that the reported ETF outflows caused changes in tokenized finance. To establish such a link would require a separate dataset and analysis. This report therefore keeps the narrower finding: the named ETF categories experienced net withdrawals during the stated period.
