## How to read the signal

A single market headline is less useful than the transmission mechanism behind it. RWA Wire therefore watches four follow-through indicators: spot liquidity in Bitcoin and Ethereum, institutional fund flows, derivatives positioning and whether risk appetite spills into tokenized-asset markets. Those indicators help distinguish a short-lived price move from a broader change in market conditions.

Tokenized assets do not trade in isolation. Changes in crypto liquidity, funding conditions and institutional positioning can affect demand for onchain collateral, stablecoins and tokenized investment products. The relevant question is not simply whether crypto prices rise or fall, but whether capital is becoming easier or harder to deploy across digital-asset infrastructure.
