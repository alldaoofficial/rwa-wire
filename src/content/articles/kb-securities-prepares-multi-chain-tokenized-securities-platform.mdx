KB Securities outlined its approach to tokenized securities at an Ethereum Korea policy seminar in Seoul on November 23, according to separate reports by EDAILY and Byline Network. Park Jae-man, who leads the brokerage's AI and digital division, said the company is designing infrastructure that can accommodate different blockchain environments rather than relying on one network.

EDAILY reported that the brokerage has selected a money market fund as its initial tokenized product and is preparing offerings for domestic and international markets. Park also identified continuous settlement as an industry challenge. Byline Network reported that he emphasized the difficulty of coordinating round-the-clock systems across multiple securities firms and described artificial intelligence as important for migrating existing business logic into smart contracts. The remarks describe preparations and operational goals, not a completed commercial platform launch.
