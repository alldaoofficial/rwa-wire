HSBC has opened access to a tokenized class of its Hong Kong dollar money market fund for eligible customers using the bank's RedCoin stablecoin, according to reports published on November 19. The minimum subscription is HK$100, paid with 100 RedCoin through the HSBC HK mobile app. This extends RedCoin beyond its initial payments role following the bank's October 23 launch on PayMe.

The tokenized Class SD belongs to HSBC Global Money Funds – Hong Kong Dollar rather than representing a newly created investment portfolio. Ethereum is the initial public network used to represent units, but participation is limited to approved wallets. The fund's official ownership register and final settlement remain off-chain, and investors cannot trade the units on a secondary market. Instead, subscriptions and redemptions use authorized distributors.

The underlying fund focuses on Hong Kong dollar short-term deposits and high-quality money market instruments. Its capital-preservation objective is not a guarantee against loss. The structure illustrates a retail-facing use of digital fund units while retaining conventional custody, compliance and settlement arrangements.
