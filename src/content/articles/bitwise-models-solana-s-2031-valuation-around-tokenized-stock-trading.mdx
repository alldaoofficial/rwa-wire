Bitwise published a Solana valuation study on November 16, outlining scenarios rather than a prediction of guaranteed returns. Its bullish 2031 model values SOL at $1,100, compared with $385 in the base case and $80 in the bearish case, according to reporting by The Block. The optimistic outcome assumes 5% of global equity trading is tokenized and Solana captures 65% of that volume, equivalent to about $30.7 trillion in annual activity. The same scenario requires approximately 3.6 billion network transactions per day, far above the level described in the research. Separately, TokenPost reports that Bitwise presented the white paper at the Solana Breakpoint conference on November 16 and cited a 42% Solana share of tokenized-equity trading volume over the first nine months of 2026. These figures describe a narrow emerging market, not all global stock trading. Adoption, rival networks, processing capacity and actual fee capture remain major uncertainties. Bitwise also issues a Solana staking ETF, a commercial interest readers should consider when assessing its research.
