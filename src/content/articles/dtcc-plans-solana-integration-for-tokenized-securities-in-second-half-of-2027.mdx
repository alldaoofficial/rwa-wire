The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation announced on November 12 that it plans to connect its Tokenization Service to the Solana blockchain. The company expects eligible securities held at its Depository Trust Company subsidiary to become available in tokenized form on Solana during the second half of 2027. This is a planned integration, not a completed Solana launch.

DTCC launched its Tokenization Service on October 28, 2026. The proposed Solana connection is intended to support conversion of eligible traditional holdings into tokenized representations and related token lifecycle functions. DTCC describes the expansion as part of a multi-chain strategy linking existing securities infrastructure with blockchain networks.

According to DTCC, securities represented through the service are intended to preserve the ownership rights, investor protections and regulatory safeguards of their traditionally held counterparts. The announcement does not establish that these assets are already trading or settling on Solana. The key milestone to monitor is delivery of the network integration in 2027.
