The Block published research on 8 October examining fragmentation in tokenized-stock markets and the role that ve(3,3) decentralized exchanges might play in directing liquidity incentives.

The report describes overlapping exposure to the same companies through different token providers, chains and venues. It discusses Aerodrome as an example of the exchange model under examination.

## What the thesis does—and does not—show

The research concerns where trading liquidity might be encouraged to gather. It does not establish that the proposed mechanism has solved fragmentation or that differently issued tokens become interchangeable.

RWA Wire's assessment is that incentives and product structure need separate evaluation. A pool can attract activity without resolving what its tokens legally represent or whether that activity persists after incentives change. The useful question raised by the report is therefore whether a particular venue can support durable trading in a defined product, rather than whether a protocol design guarantees adoption.
