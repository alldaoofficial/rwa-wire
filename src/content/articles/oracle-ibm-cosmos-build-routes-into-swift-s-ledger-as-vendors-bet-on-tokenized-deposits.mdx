Oracle and Cosmos announced routes to Swift's shared ledger on 28 September, following an IBM announcement on 24 September. The proposals connect banks' existing systems with tokenized-deposit infrastructure rather than requiring all banks to adopt one replacement core ledger.

Oracle describes coordinated payment flows across institution-controlled deposit systems. IBM describes a beta ISO 20022 adapter in Digital Asset Haven and an on-premises beta option. Cosmos describes tools for connecting bank cores to tokenized-deposit ledgers and other networks.

## Different announcements, different stages

The vendors are describing integration capabilities. IBM's beta label is particularly important: it identifies a testing stage, not unconditional production availability for every institution.

For this story, the concrete change is the availability or development of interfaces between existing payment operations and new ledgers. It does not show that the three products have identical functionality or that banks using them have all completed customer-facing deployment.
