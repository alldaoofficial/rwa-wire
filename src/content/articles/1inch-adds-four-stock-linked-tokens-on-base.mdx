1inch has expanded the selection of stock-linked tokens accessible on Base, adding instruments associated with Microsoft, Advanced Micro Devices, Sony and Take-Two Interactive. TokenPost reported the four additions on November 14, while Coinfomania separately described the new batch and linked to a November announcement by the 1inch account. The tokens are presented as available through the 1inch decentralized application and wallet, rather than through a conventional stock brokerage interface.

The reports describe trading access around the clock, a feature that differs from the regular session of traditional equity exchanges. The expansion should not be confused with the initial introduction of stock-linked tokens to Base: it adds names to an existing offering. Neither report establishes trading volume, consistent liquidity, universal geographic availability or the legal rights conveyed by each token. Those details require issuer-specific documentation before users can equate the instruments with directly held shares.
