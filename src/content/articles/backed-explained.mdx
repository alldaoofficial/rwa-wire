Backed is an issuer focused on representing traditional financial securities through blockchain-based products.

## Tokenized equities

Its current product database contains a broad catalogue of xStocks linked to publicly traded companies. Each product is documented with instrument-specific legal materials, identifiers and smart-contract information.

## Where it fits

Backed sits at the issuance layer of tokenized capital markets. Rather than building a general-purpose blockchain, it creates regulated instruments that can be distributed across blockchain ecosystems and integrated into compatible applications.

## Important distinction

A token linked to a public equity should not automatically be treated as identical to holding the underlying share through a conventional brokerage account. Legal structure, holder rights, transfer restrictions, redemption mechanics and jurisdictional availability need to be checked for the specific instrument.
