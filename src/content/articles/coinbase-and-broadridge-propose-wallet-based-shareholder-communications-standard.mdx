Coinbase and Broadridge announced on November 16 that they had published a proposed open technical standard for sending shareholder communications to wallets holding tokenized shares. The proposal was also submitted for review as an Ethereum Improvement Proposal, meaning it should not be described as a finalized Ethereum-wide requirement.

The companies say the framework is intended for issuers, custodians and wallet providers that need a common way to deliver investor materials across different tokenization arrangements. Broadridge separately launched a data oracle intended to provide participating firms with aggregate evidence that communications have been distributed. That evidence function is distinct from establishing the legal rights attached to any particular token.

Broadridge says its existing onchain governance offering already connects ProxyVote with digital wallets, enabling access to materials, holding verification and voting. The partners plan to explore additional corporate actions beyond their current communications and voting work. They also caution that practical implementation depends on share structures, intermediaries and applicable legal requirements. The announcement therefore marks a proposed interoperability framework and an oracle launch, not universal adoption by tokenized-stock issuers.
