## South Korea: a phased roadmap

South Korea's Financial Services Commission published a token-securities roadmap on September 4, 2026. Its staged approach begins with specified private-market and institutional activity before broader expansion. The roadmap describes an implementation path; it should not be read as evidence that every proposed market segment was already live in September.

## United States: a conditional venue framework

A September SEC order addresses exemptions for certain tokenized-securities venues and related participants. It is a defined framework with conditions, not an unrestricted authorization for any stock token or trading platform.

## Why these developments belong apart

One document sets out a market-development sequence. The other addresses regulatory treatment of particular venue arrangements. Combining both under a single claim that global rules have converged would lose their differences.

For each jurisdiction, the useful next check is the actual implementation document and the qualifying participants or products. This brief covers these two verified documents and does not infer equivalent changes in other countries.
