J.P. Morgan Asset Management has expanded two tokenized money market funds to Solana. My OnChain Net Yield Fund (MONY) and JPMorgan OnChain Liquidity–Token Money Market Fund (JLTXX) are now available on the network to qualified U.S. investors, according to the firm's October 22 announcement.

MONY is a Rule 506(c) private-placement fund offering access to U.S. dollar yields. JLTXX is a registered government money market fund designed to invest in a way that supports stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act. The release did not disclose assets deployed on Solana.

Investors use J.P. Morgan's Morgan Money platform to subscribe and redeem, then receive token balances at their blockchain addresses. The expansion is a multi-chain distribution step for existing funds, not a retail product launch or a prediction about the price of SOL.
