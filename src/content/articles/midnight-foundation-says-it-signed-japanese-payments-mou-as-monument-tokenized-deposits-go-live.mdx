Midnight Foundation executive Fahmi Syed said the organization has signed a memorandum of understanding with a major Japanese payments company, according to a November 19 interview published by NADA NEWS. The company was not identified, and the agreement should not be mistaken for a live Japanese banking or payments deployment.

Syed also described meetings in Tokyo with banks and businesses handling intellectual property and medical information. NADA NEWS reported that the foundation maintains a local representative in Japan, one of its priority markets. The interview indicated that prospective partners are watching how Monument Bank's deployment performs before making further commitments.

That UK rollout provides the operational context. In a November 17 announcement, Midnight said Monument had introduced tokenized retail savings deposits through its existing banking app. The fixed-term product offers 5.2% annual equivalent rate for 12 months, with up to £250 million of deposits targeted for one-to-one sterling representation on Midnight in the initial phase. The foundation says customers need not manage crypto wallets or network fees. Japan's memorandum remains an early-stage agreement, distinct from the already launched UK product.
