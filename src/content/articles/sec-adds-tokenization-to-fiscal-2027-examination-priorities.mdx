The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission added tokenization to the Division of Examinations' fiscal 2027 priorities on October 23. The annual plan guides a risk-based program covering more than 34,000 registrants, but the SEC says it is not an exhaustive list of examination topics.

Tokenization appears in two market-infrastructure areas. Examinations of clearing agencies will review it alongside operations, securities lending, default management, recovery and wind-down. Reviews under Regulation Systems Compliance and Integrity will also focus on tokenized securities, extended trading hours, advanced computing and technology-vendor risk.

The document does not announce a new rule or enforcement action. It signals where examiners expect heightened operational, governance and investor-protection risks as regulated firms adopt tokenized securities infrastructure.
