DBS and Kinexys by JPMorgan announced on November 16, 2026, that institutional clients can transfer tokenized US dollar deposits between blockchain-based accounts in Singapore and the United States around the clock. The launched service links DBS Token Services with Kinexys's permissioned Blockchain Deposit Account network, turning an interoperability framework announced in November 2025 into an operational cross-bank connection.

Ant International is the first client to use the arrangement for treasury and liquidity management across entities in both countries. The banks say Partior provides settlement orchestration, while their collaboration also contributes to the Monetary Authority of Singapore's Project BLOOM interoperability work.

The round-the-clock description has an important qualification: transfers within Kinexys's blockchain deposit network run continuously, but moving money between traditional demand deposit accounts and blockchain deposit accounts still faces a three-hour interruption each Saturday, from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time. The banks say an enhancement is being developed. This is a service for eligible institutions using bank deposits, not a public cryptocurrency transfer network.
