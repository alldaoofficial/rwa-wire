Invesco announced the launch of its Stablecoin Reserves Onchain Fund, ticker IROXX, on November 17, 2026. The actively managed government money market fund is designed for institutional investors seeking to hold assets backing payment stablecoins in a tokenized format. Invesco says its investment strategy is structured around reserve assets eligible under the U.S. GENIUS Act.

The portfolio focuses on short-duration U.S. Treasury securities and other permitted reserve instruments. Superstate supplies its FundOS tokenization platform, including blockchain-integrated transfer agency and ownership recordkeeping. Invesco's Global Liquidity team manages the portfolio. The structure supports transactions in U.S. dollars and USDC, while dividends are reinvested daily.

IROXX is organized as a registered Rule 2a-7 government money market fund within the Invesco Short-Term Investments Trust. Initial investors are expected to include stablecoin issuers and digital-asset infrastructure providers. Invesco also identifies IROXX as the first publicly registered fund launched on Superstate's FundOS platform.

The development links traditional cash management with onchain records, but tokenization does not remove investment risk. Invesco cautions that the fund is not FDIC insured, may lose value and faces blockchain operational risks. Its shares represent interests in a fund holding reserve assets, not investments in stablecoins.
