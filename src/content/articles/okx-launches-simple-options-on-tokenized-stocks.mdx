OKX launched Simple Options on November 4 for eligible customers across Southeast Asia, Northeast Asia, the CIS region, MENA and Türkiye. The initial underlyings are tokenized NVDA, SPCX, MU and GOOGL, with more planned. The European-style contracts let users buy or sell calls and puts through an over-the-counter request-for-quote process.

Positions are fully funded rather than margined. Buyers pay the premium upfront, while sellers set aside tokenized shares or USDT, so the product does not use liquidation for insufficient margin. Trading and quote requests are available 24/7. The minimum size is 0.01 contract, representing one tokenized share, while a standard contract represents 100 shares. Premiums and cash settlement are denominated in USDT. Contracts settle at 16:00 Hong Kong time on expiry, and positions can be closed earlier when a quote is available before the cutoff.
