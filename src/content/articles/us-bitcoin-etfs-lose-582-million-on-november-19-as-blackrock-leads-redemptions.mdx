US-listed spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds registered approximately $582.3 million in net outflows on November 19, 2026, according to the daily fund-level table published by Farside Investors. The result marked a third consecutive negative trading session, following roughly $239.9 million in withdrawals on November 17 and $306.5 million on November 18.

BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust, or IBIT, accounted for $541.1 million of the November 19 net withdrawals. Fidelity's FBTC lost $45.9 million, Ark 21Shares' ARKB lost $31.3 million and Grayscale's GBTC lost $13.9 million. The pattern was not universal: Morgan Stanley's MSBT attracted $49.6 million, while WisdomTree's BTCW added about $0.3 million, according to Farside's table.

The Block reported that cumulative November net outflows had reached approximately $3.47 billion through November 19. That is a flow measure, not a measure of Bitcoin price losses or proof that every ETF holder sold. Differences between individual funds are important when assessing demand for listed Bitcoin exposure.
