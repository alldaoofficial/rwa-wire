The Hong Kong Monetary Authority said on November 16, 2026, that it is strengthening its collaboration with Swift to investigate cross-border payments using tokenized bank deposits. The work will explore interoperability between Swift's ledger and the settlement layer of Hong Kong's Project Ensemble, with an emphasis on deposits denominated in multiple currencies.

The HKMA named Bank of China (Hong Kong), Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong) and HSBC among the banking participants. The regulator said it has worked with Swift since 2023 on interoperability between central bank digital currency systems, including activity through the Project Ensemble sandbox.

The new announcement describes an exploratory collaboration, not the launch of a commercially available cross-border payment rail. Its objective is to connect local tokenized settlement infrastructure with Swift's international network, potentially improving how regulated institutions move money across borders. The technical integration, operating model and timing of any broader deployment were not specified in the release.
