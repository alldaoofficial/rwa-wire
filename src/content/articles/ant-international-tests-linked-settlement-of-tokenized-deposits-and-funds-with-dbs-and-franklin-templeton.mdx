Ant International announced the Whale Orchestration Protocol on November 19, 2026, as an upgrade to its WhaleRTP blockchain-based wholesale settlement platform. The protocol is designed to link tokenized bank deposits with tokenized money market fund shares through delivery-versus-payment transactions.

The company said it had completed pilot transactions with DBS and Franklin Templeton. DBS integrated its Treasury Tokens, while Franklin Templeton contributed its tokenized money market fund solution. The participants described the tests as Singapore's first successful cross-asset interoperability between these two types of tokenized instruments.

The work was developed under the Monetary Authority of Singapore's BLOOM initiative, which focuses on interoperable digital-money arrangements. The three firms also released a whitepaper explaining the protocol and its relationship to the BLOOM Field Guide.

For corporate treasury teams, the proposed benefit is a closer connection between payment balances and short-term investments without waiting for conventional fund-settlement windows. However, the announced milestone concerns completed pilot transactions; the release does not establish a general customer launch or quantify live transaction volumes.
