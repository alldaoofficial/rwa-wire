Kaia Investment Partners added a loan to ONiON Mobility, an electric-vehicle operator in Cambodia, to its tokenized private-credit fund Yield8. The position is the fund's fifth investment and its first mobility exposure. MVL's mobility-finance platform Musubi facilitated the transaction.

ONiON Mobility's electric-vehicle lease revenue is intended to repay the loan. Yield8 investors receive reporting linked to a pool of lease invoices, but those invoices are provided for disclosure and are not collateral. A USDC payment reserve is locked in a smart contract to support distributions. The reserve only covers the amount deposited and does not guarantee repayment of principal or investment returns. Kaia Investment Partners says it may evaluate further Musubi-originated loans, but any additional allocations remain prospective rather than committed portfolio positions.
