Chainlink and LG CNS announced a strategic partnership on October 22 to support regulated digital-asset infrastructure in Korea. The companies said Chainlink's cross-chain, data and orchestration capabilities will be applied to LG CNS's Digital Asset Platform Solution. They also plan to develop technical standards and solutions for Korean financial institutions.

The stated scope includes interoperability between systems and networks, access to external data and potential infrastructure for tokenized money and securities. LG CNS described the work as supporting institutional adoption, while Chainlink framed it as an expansion of its Korean presence. The announcement establishes a collaboration and development agenda; it does not document the launch of a commercial stablecoin, a tokenized-security issuance or a production service for customers.
