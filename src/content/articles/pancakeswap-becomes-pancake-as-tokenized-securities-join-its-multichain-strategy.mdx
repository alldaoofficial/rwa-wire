PancakeSwap announced on November 18 that it is adopting the shorter name Pancake, positioning the decentralized finance platform as a broader multichain trading and liquidity service. The company says it began in 2020 as a token-swapping venue and now operates across more than 11 blockchain networks, including BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Solana and Ethereum.

The rebranded offering groups products into trading, earning and developer services. Its Stock Terminal already provides access to tokenized stocks and exchange-traded funds around the clock, alongside token swaps and perpetual futures. Other offerings include CAKE staking through Syrup Pools, Dual Investment and tools for developers to integrate liquidity and aggregation features.

In its announcement, the company reported more than 208 million users and over $4.4 trillion in cumulative trading volume. Those figures describe the overall platform, not tokenized-securities activity alone. Pancake also said it intends to release a mobile application in the coming weeks; that is a plan rather than a completed launch.

For the tokenization market, the branding shift highlights how decentralized trading venues are integrating representations of traditional securities into wider product menus. It does not itself establish new securities approvals or prove that tokenized-equity demand has increased.
