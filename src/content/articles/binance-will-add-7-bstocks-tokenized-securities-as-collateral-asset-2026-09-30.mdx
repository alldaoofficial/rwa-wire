## What Binance announced

Binance announced seven additional bStocks as collateral assets on September 30, 2026. The change concerns the use of supported tokenized securities within its margin infrastructure. It does not mean that every tokenized stock can now secure a loan on every exchange.

## Why the distinction matters

Collateral support gives an instrument another possible use beyond buying and selling it. A trader may be able to retain exposure while using an eligible asset to support positions. The practical value depends on the platform's collateral rules, valuation adjustments and liquidation policy.

Those questions are separate from the legal instrument behind a bStock. Readers should check the product documentation for the claim the certificate provides, rather than infer ordinary shareholder rights from a familiar company ticker.

The useful follow-up is whether eligibility translates into actual collateral use. An announcement of support establishes availability; it supplies no evidence about outstanding borrowing or the number of users taking up that option.
