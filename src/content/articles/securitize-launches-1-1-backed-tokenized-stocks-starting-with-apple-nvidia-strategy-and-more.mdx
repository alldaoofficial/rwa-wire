Securitize announced its stock-token offering on 8 October. The initial launch uses Solana and the company's registered broker-dealer platform, with tokens backed by underlying shares for eligible investors.

The stated structure is a security entitlement under UCC Article 8. Conversion into directly registered shares is described as available where the relevant arrangements permit it. The two forms of holding should not be conflated.

## Available now versus planned

The announcement separates the initial platform from expected access through the NYSE's proposed venue and OKXICE. Those routes depend on their launch and regulatory or operational requirements.

For this story, the material change is the launch of a share-backed product through existing regulated infrastructure. A planned connection is not proof of an operating venue, and token backing alone is not a statement that every holder appears directly on the issuer's share register. This consolidated report replaces several overlapping launch summaries.
