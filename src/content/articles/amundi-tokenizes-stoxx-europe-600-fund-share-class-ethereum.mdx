Amundi launched a tokenized share class of its Core STOXX Europe 600 fund on November 5, extending its digital-asset program from cash-management products into European equity index investing. The new share class was issued on the public Ethereum blockchain and was developed with CACEIS, which provides the technology and infrastructure for tokenizing the fund units.

The underlying fund remains part of Amundi's existing operating model and continues to be available through traditional distribution channels. The tokenized share class therefore adds a blockchain-based representation and distribution route rather than replacing the conventional fund. Amundi presents the launch as part of a phased strategy and says it intends to develop more tokenized funds for institutional clients and digital platforms. That broader range remains an ambition, not a completed product rollout.
