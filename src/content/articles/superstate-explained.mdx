Superstate builds regulated tokenization infrastructure intended to connect traditional securities with blockchain-based capital markets.

## Fund infrastructure

Its FundOS platform is designed for asset managers seeking to tokenize existing or new investment funds. Superstate also manages products including USTB and USCC, giving its platform direct exposure to both government-securities and crypto-related investment strategies.

## Public equities

Opening Bell extends the model toward public-company shares, with Superstate describing the product as infrastructure for issuing official tokenized shares on Ethereum and Solana.

## Market role

Superstate therefore sits across both asset management and tokenization infrastructure. The blockchain representation changes distribution and settlement mechanics, but investors remain exposed to the terms and risks of the underlying regulated security.
