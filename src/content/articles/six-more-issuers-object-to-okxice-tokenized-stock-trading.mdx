OKXICE updated its public notice on October 23 to identify six additional issuers that objected to tokenized trading of their shares on the planned venue. Arm Holdings, Wells Fargo, Moderna, Cisco Systems, Intuit and Marvell Technology joined the 16 issuers listed in the October 19 version, taking the disclosed total to 22.

The objections concern tokenized shares created by an unaffiliated third party and offered through OKXICE under the SEC's temporary framework. They prevent those instruments from trading on this venue; they do not prohibit issuer-sponsored tokens or another venue operating under a separate notice. OKXICE remains a planned venue, not a confirmed live market. Its notice describes permissioned Uniswap v4 pools on XLayer, participant screening and one-for-one share backing for third-party tokens, but it gives no launch date or reasons for the individual objections.
