Dune launched Market Data Feeds on October 22, giving institutional teams normalized real-time trade data from more than 200 onchain venues across 12 blockchains. The spot feed includes networks such as Ethereum, Solana, Base, BNB Chain and XLayer, while the perpetuals feed initially covers Hyperliquid and Lighter. Each record includes price, size, trade side, a stable identifier and a link to the settling transaction.

Dune said tokenized stocks and ETFs generated more than $37 billion of onchain trading volume in the third quarter of 2026, with monthly volume almost 18 times January’s level. It separately reported $201 billion of Q3 volume in stock, ETF and equity-index perpetuals on Hyperliquid. Those figures are company-reported market measurements, not asset values or forecasts. Historical analytical data is offered separately for backtesting.
