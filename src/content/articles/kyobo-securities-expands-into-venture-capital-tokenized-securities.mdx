Seoul Economic Daily reported on 11 October that Kyobo Securities was expanding its venture-capital activity while exploring security-token offerings and real-world assets.

The report describes investments in technology companies, including digital-asset infrastructure, alongside research into asset-backed tokenization. It says the brokerage is considering structures involving physical assets and content intellectual-property revenues, as well as distribution-platform connections.

## Two separate businesses

A venture investment gives a brokerage exposure to a company. A security-token offering creates or distributes a financial instrument for investors. The former should not be counted as evidence that the latter is already available.

Kyobo's distribution plans are also described as preparatory. The report does not establish customer access, an issued instrument or transaction volume for a newly launched tokenized product. The supported reading is a diversification strategy with tokenization work under development, rather than a completed commercial rollout.
