Temple Digital Group has engaged tZERO Securities to operate the regulated broker-dealer and alternative trading system layer for Temple's planned tokenized securities platform on the Canton Network. Temple will pair its proprietary trading stack with tZERO's client onboarding and transaction services.

Temple's technology includes a matching engine, request-for-quote module and central limit order book designed for tokenized National Market System securities and exchange-traded products. The company also describes settlement infrastructure supporting atomic delivery-versus-payment and repo transactions on Canton. tZERO Securities, an SEC-registered broker-dealer and FINRA/SIPC member, is expected to operate the dedicated venue. This is an infrastructure agreement, not a completed launch: implementation remains pending, and the companies said launch timing and product availability will be announced separately.
