Ondo Finance announced on October 29 that seven Intelligent Portfolios are live on Solana for eligible investors outside the United States. Each product is a single token that provides exposure to a defined portfolio of Ondo Stocks rather than requiring the holder to buy and rebalance every constituent separately. The underlying positions are tokenized equities and exchange-traded funds backed by securities, according to Ondo.

The lineup includes three income products, two growth products and two thematic products. Three of the seven use portfolio strategies developed by BlackRock for Ondo: BLKHIon, BLKGRWon and BLKDIGon. The other tokens target specified income levels, a Magnificent 7 and crypto allocation, or an artificial-intelligence theme. BlackRock's role concerns the strategies; Ondo implements and distributes the tokenized products.

Ondo says allocation, rebalancing and fee logic are encoded in smart contracts, with rebalancing performed under preset schedules. Holdings, weights and rebalances are visible onchain. The company reported that the earlier Ethereum and BNB Chain versions had exceeded $45 million in total value locked during their first weeks of trading. That historical figure is Ondo's October 29 report, not a forecast for the Solana expansion.
