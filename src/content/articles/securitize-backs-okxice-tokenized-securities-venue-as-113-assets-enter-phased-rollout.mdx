Securitize announced on November 23 that it is providing tokenization infrastructure for the newly launched OKXICE Tokenized Securities Venue, a joint venture of OKX and Intercontinental Exchange, the parent of the New York Stock Exchange. According to the company's release, the venue has confirmed 113 assets for a phased rollout, spanning stocks, exchange-traded funds and other exchange-traded products. This is a planned asset lineup, not a claim that all instruments are immediately tradable.

Securitize says each token represents a security entitlement backed one-for-one by an underlying share, with applicable economic and governance rights passed through under governing terms. Trading uses Uniswap v4 automated market-maker pools on X Layer, while smart contracts restrict participation to eligible wallets. The venue says it does not custody customers' assets or private keys.

Access is currently limited to eligible U.S.-based OKX Inc. customers who complete identity verification and venue onboarding. The company describes its regulatory basis as a temporary, conditional SEC Innovation Exemption; the release expressly says the SEC has not approved or endorsed the venue. Securitize also warns that 24/7 availability does not guarantee continuous liquidity and that prices may diverge from underlying shares when traditional exchanges are closed.
