Plume is a blockchain ecosystem built around real-world asset finance, or RWAfi. Its thesis is that bringing an asset onchain is only one part of tokenization; the asset also needs distribution, liquidity and applications that can use it.

## Infrastructure model

Plume combines an EVM-compatible execution environment with infrastructure intended to simplify the onboarding and management of tokenized assets. Its ecosystem is designed to let those assets interact with lending, trading and other onchain applications.

## Where it fits

This places Plume at the blockchain and distribution layer rather than in the role of a single asset issuer. The network can host products representing different underlying asset categories.

## What to watch

A purpose-built RWA ecosystem still depends on issuer quality, legal enforceability, liquidity and the security of the applications built around each asset. Network activity alone does not establish the quality of an underlying investment.
