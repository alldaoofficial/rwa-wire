ADI Foundation and Securitize announced on 30 September that Securitize's tokenization infrastructure would integrate with ADI Chain. The announcement frames the work as an expansion of Securitize's multichain infrastructure.

It describes a pathway for eligible financial products to use ADI Chain, subject to regulatory, product and jurisdictional requirements. It does not supply a universal launch date or say that every existing fund is moving to the network.

## What an integration establishes

Adding a supported network concerns the rails on which a product may operate. The financial instrument still has its own issuer, transfer rules and eligible investor base. Those conditions cannot be inferred from the blockchain announcement alone.

The relevant next evidence would be a named product deploying through the integration. Until then, the supported conclusion is narrower: the companies are creating another infrastructure option for institutional tokenization, rather than announcing unrestricted access to all tokenized assets.
