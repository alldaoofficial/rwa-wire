## The interview

The Wall Street Journal published an October 9, 2026 interview with Cuomo, co-chair of OKX–ICE, about the potential for tokenized stocks to change financial markets. The comments describe an outlook from someone involved in building a proposed tokenized trading venue.

## How to read the forecast

A venture participant can explain the opportunity it is pursuing. That perspective is useful, but it does not by itself demonstrate that investors have adopted the product or that a new market structure has displaced existing exchanges.

The practical evidence will come from the service's operating arrangements and subsequent activity: what instruments it lists, who can use it, and whether customers find usable liquidity.

Readers should therefore keep the forecast separate from a launch announcement or a regulatory determination. The interview contributes a view of where tokenized equities might go. It supplies no basis for treating that destination as an already completed change.
