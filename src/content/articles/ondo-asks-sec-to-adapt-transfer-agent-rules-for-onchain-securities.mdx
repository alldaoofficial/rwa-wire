Ondo Finance submitted a November 3 comment letter on the SEC's proposed transfer-agent rule overhaul. The filing supports modernization but asks the agency to preserve distinct issuer, intermediary and linked-security records, recognize authorized agents serving investors and provide predictable electronic connections to issuers' transfer agents.

Ondo also argues that a transfer agent can control legally effective record changes without controlling a public blockchain's consensus. It recommends protected offchain identity records for routine downstream activity, documented program-level legal reviews when material facts remain unchanged, and transaction-level escalation when facts or risks change.

The SEC proposal would amend transfer-agent rules and forms, add two rules and rescind one existing rule. Ondo's letter is advocacy submitted during that rulemaking; it does not change current law or establish that the SEC will adopt the requested framework.
