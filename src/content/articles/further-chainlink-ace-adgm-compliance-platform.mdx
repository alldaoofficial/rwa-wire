Further Asset Management announced on October 27 that it has integrated Chainlink's Automated Compliance Engine into Anubis, its platform for machine-readable regulatory workflows. The initial deployment focuses on tokenized-asset compliance within Abu Dhabi Global Market. Anubis evaluates transactions against configured policies before execution, with controls designed to support applicable Financial Services Regulatory Authority requirements.

The initial rule sets cover areas including issuance, customer identification, anti-money-laundering checks, ownership, trading, custody and sanctions. Further says Chainlink ACE supports consistent policy application and traceable compliance decisions while allowing rules to change without redeploying token contracts. These are technical controls configured around regulatory requirements; the announcement does not state that ADGM or the FSRA approved Anubis or any tokenized asset.

Further plans to expand Anubis to more jurisdictions and counterparties. A planned dashboard would give virtual-asset service providers and regulators visibility into how supported transactions are assessed. Further and Chainlink said additional milestones will follow as institutions begin adopting the platform, so broader deployment remains prospective.
