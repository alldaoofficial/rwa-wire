Ondo Finance announced on October 27 that 15 of its tokenized stocks and ETFs are now available on Robinhood Chain through the Ondo Bridge. Users can bridge the assets from Ethereum or BNB Chain, where Ondo supports minting and redemption. The rollout is distinct from the existing Morpho lending markets for Robinhood-issued stock tokens.

Eleven of the Ondo assets have Uniswap v4 StablePair pools against Robinhood Stock Tokens referencing the same securities. The paired list includes tokens linked to Nvidia, Apple, Tesla, Meta, Alphabet, Strategy, Circle, GameStop, SpaceX, the SPDR S&P 500 ETF and the Invesco QQQ ETF. Four additional Ondo assets are live without corresponding Robinhood-token pairs.

Ondo says its stock tokens can be minted and redeemed around the clock. That creates a potential weekend route for eligible traders when Robinhood Stock Tokens continue trading but Robinhood's minting and redemption window is closed. The StablePair design adjusts liquidity-provider fees when paired tokens diverge from their expected exchange rate. Availability remains subject to jurisdictional and other restrictions.
