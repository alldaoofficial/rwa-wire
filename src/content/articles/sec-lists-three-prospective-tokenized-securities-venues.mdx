The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission updated its Innovation Exemption page on October 21 to identify three parties that submitted written notices of intent to operate Tokenized Securities Venues. TSV LLC filed on September 23, OKXICE TSV on October 4, and Kephas Corporation, doing business as Perpetuals.com, on October 14.

The roster is a disclosure milestone, not an approval list. The SEC says inclusion does not establish that a person qualifies as a Tokenized Securities Venue or satisfies the exemption's conditions. The underlying September 17 order provides temporary, conditional relief for qualifying venues that use permissioned automated-market-maker liquidity pools to trade tokenized NMS stocks. None of the three entries, by itself, proves that customer trading has launched. The new consolidated list adds TSV LLC to the public picture while confirming the dates already associated with OKXICE and Perpetuals.com notices.
