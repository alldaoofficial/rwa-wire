The US Securities and Exchange Commission announced on November 25 that its Investor Advisory Committee will convene online on December 3 at 9:30 a.m. Eastern time. One session will examine the tokenization of shares in American public companies in connection with the agency's innovation exemption.

The agenda also includes separate discussions on insolvency and financial reporting supervision. Members are expected to consider possible recommendations concerning the trade-through rule and research into individual investors' understanding of private assets.

The committee provides advice to the regulator rather than making binding rules. Market participants should therefore distinguish the scheduled discussion from a formal decision permitting tokenized equity trading.
