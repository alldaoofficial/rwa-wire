Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission approved secondary trading of two existing tokenized public money market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual asset trading platform. Around-the-clock trading began on October 26, 2026, for the ChinaAMC HKD Digital Money Market Fund and ChinaAMC USD Digital Money Market Fund.

The approval implements a framework introduced in April 2026 for piloting secondary trading of SFC-authorized tokenized funds. The regulator says the safeguards cover operation of the trading channel, fair pricing, liquidity provision and disclosure. This is a live secondary-market launch, not merely an authorization for future trading.

The first batch is limited to tokenized money market funds. The SFC said it will monitor trading and may consider expanding the eligible product scope later; that expansion remains prospective. The regulator also identified potential future links with tokenized deposits developed under Project Ensemble, but did not announce that integration as operational.
