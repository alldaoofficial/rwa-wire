Chainalysis reported on October 14 that Japan represented 16.4% of identifiable tokenized U.S. Treasury holders but only 0.4% of the corresponding value. Italy accounted for 29.9% of geolocated value, Portugal for 18.8% and Singapore for 18.2%, a combined 66.9%. The United States ranked fourth by value at 10.5%.

The figures cover Chainalysis's 12-month reporting period ending July 1, 2026, rather than a current market snapshot. The firm measured $15.42 billion of tokenized U.S. Treasury value, up 125% from $6.86 billion. Chainalysis interprets the holder-value gap as evidence that a relatively small group of large foreign holders drives much of the market while a broader retail base holds smaller positions.
