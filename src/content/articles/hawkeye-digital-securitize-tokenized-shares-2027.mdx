Hawkeye Digital announced on November 6 that it has agreed with Securitize Transfer Agent to develop an optional program for tokenizing its common stock, initially on Ethereum. The announcement was furnished as an exhibit to Hawkeye's Form 8-K filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission.

Under the proposed structure, eligible shareholders would hold the same HWKE common stock that trades on OTC Markets, not a synthetic instrument or a wrapped price-tracking token. Hawkeye says the tokenized shares would preserve existing shareholder rights, including voting and dividends. The tokenized holdings would be recorded onchain and reconciled with the company's official shareholder register.

Participating investors would first move their shares from brokerage custody into direct registration with the transfer agent. They would then complete identity-verification and anti-money-laundering checks before converting their holdings through Securitize. Conversion back to book-entry form is also contemplated, subject to applicable procedures.

The company expects tokenized HWKE shares to become available in 2027. The announcement does not establish that the service is already live or open to all investors. Traditional OTC trading in HWKE is expected to continue, and participation in tokenization would remain optional. The planned launch and operational features are forward-looking and subject to implementation and regulatory risks.
