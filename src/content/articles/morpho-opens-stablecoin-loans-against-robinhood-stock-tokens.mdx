Morpho has opened lending markets on Robinhood Chain that use selected Robinhood Stock Tokens as collateral for stablecoin loans. The protocol's live dashboard lists stock-linked collateral including NVDA, GOOGL, AAPL and SPCX, with USDG used across several displayed markets. Independent reports dated October 22 and 23 confirm that eligible users can borrow stablecoins without selling the tokens and that applications can connect to the markets.

The tokens provide economic exposure to referenced shares; they do not confer legal or beneficial ownership of the underlying companies. Borrowers also face DeFi-specific risks. A position can be liquidated when debt exceeds its market threshold, and equity-linked oracle updates may follow market schedules even while onchain activity continues. The launch is therefore a collateral integration, not a conventional securities-backed bank loan.
