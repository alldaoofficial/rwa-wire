OKXICE disclosed on October 15 that nine issuers had objected to tokenized versions of their shares being offered on its planned Tokenized Securities Venue. The list comprises Cerebras Systems, AppLovin, Applied Digital, Rocket Lab, CoreWeave, Verizon, Applied Optoelectronics, Broadcom and UnitedHealth Group.

The consequence is specific to OKXICE. Under the SEC's temporary innovation exemption, a venue must notify an issuer before offering stock tokenized by an unaffiliated third party and wait at least 30 calendar days. A timely written objection prevents that venue from making the tokenized stock available. It does not prohibit another venue from starting its own notification process. OKXICE says its model would use one-for-one share backing, permissioned Uniswap v4 pools on X Layer and stablecoin trading pairs, but the nine objected securities cannot be included under the current notices.
