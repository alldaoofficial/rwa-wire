CoinNess reported on 6 October that Chainlink executive Nick Ariasinghe expected South Korea's tokenization market to accelerate from 2027, citing an interview with The Herald Business and discussions with local institutions.

The official policy context is more specific. On 1 October, Korea's Financial Services Commission proposed subordinate-rule revisions for securities tokenization, with implementation intended from 4 February 2027. The proposals entered a comment period running from 2 October to 11 November.

## A date is not an adoption measure

The regulatory timetable explains why firms are preparing infrastructure, but it does not establish how many products will launch or how much capital they will attract.

The two sources should therefore be read separately: Ariasinghe provides an industry expectation; the FSC provides a proposed framework and process. Neither alone verifies the pace of subsequent commercial adoption. This distinction replaces the previous generic claim that any institutional tokenization development necessarily signals production use.
