Seven Stars Fund CEIC opened for subscriptions from professional investors on October 27. The closed-ended DIFC vehicle will provide senior-secured, full-recourse loans to regulated UK law firms and claims management companies pursuing consumer-redress claims. Truleum Venture Partners is the DFSA-authorized manager, Apex Group is the administrator, and Tokeny supplies the ERC-3643 issuance infrastructure.

The fund targets GBP 250 million, with a GBP 400 million hard cap and a USD 50,000 minimum investment. Its sponsor is targeting a 16% annual return, but that figure is a target, not a realized yield or guarantee. EnterpriseAM reported that the fund had no commitments and had made no loans when it opened. It expects an initial close of about GBP 50 million in Q1 2027.

The launch is a material step beyond the structure announced in 2025 because subscriptions are now open within a regulated fund framework. Credit risk remains: borrowers owe repayment regardless of individual claim outcomes, but their ability to repay still depends on the performance of their claims portfolios.
