RWA.xyz ranked Ondo first among 203 distributed tokenization platforms in its November 5 snapshot. The table recorded $4,066,170,187 of Ondo real-world asset value excluding stablecoins, spread across 484 assets and 568,129 holders. Securitize ranked second with $3,969,035,764 across 50 assets and 1,493 holders.

The comparison covers platforms that use blockchain as a distribution layer, allowing investors to subscribe, hold and manage assets through wallets or custodians. It does not include RWA.xyz's separate represented-platform category, where blockchain is used for recordkeeping without onchain investor distribution. Ondo's total spans several asset classes, including Treasury products and tokenized stocks, so the overall lead should not be read as first place in every individual category. The figures are a dated market snapshot and can change with asset values, subscriptions and redemptions.
