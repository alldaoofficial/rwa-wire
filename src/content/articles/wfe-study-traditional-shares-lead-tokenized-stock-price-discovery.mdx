The World Federation of Exchanges published research on October 20 finding that traditional exchange-traded shares remained the main source of price discovery for the third-party tokenized equities in its sample. The study examined 61 matched xStock and underlying-equity pairs using daily observations from July 2025 through May 2026.

## What the study found

Around 80% of the pairs were cointegrated, meaning their prices maintained a stable long-run relationship. That relationship was close to one-for-one. When prices diverged, the token generally made more of the adjustment back toward the underlying share price.

For the median pair, the underlying equity accounted for more than 80% of the permanent component of the common price. Under the study's Hasbrouck information-share analysis, no token led price discovery.

## Limits and context

The results apply to this xStock sample, the study period and daily-frequency data. They do not establish that every tokenized-equity market will behave the same way, or that intraday trading never adds information. The WFE is also an exchange-industry association, so its institutional perspective should be considered when interpreting the conclusions.
