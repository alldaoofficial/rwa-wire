U.S. spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds had recorded almost $2.9 billion in net outflows during November by the morning of November 19, according to CoinDesk's day-ahead market report. That compares with roughly $500 million in October. The report placed the withdrawals alongside Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, but the overlap alone cannot establish where the redeemed capital went.

CoinDesk reported that traders on Hyperliquid had priced Anthropic-related perpetual contracts above $100 per share, compared with the company's $83 initial public offering price. It also cited a Grayscale review of eight earlier IPOs in which such contracts anticipated the opening direction in seven cases. Those historical observations are not guarantees of future pricing accuracy.

Separate reporting from Bitcoin.com described $306.42 million in net outflows from spot Bitcoin ETFs on Wednesday, November 18, across seven funds. The report said BlackRock's fund led the redemptions. Together, these figures point to weaker demand for listed Bitcoin exposure during a period of intense attention on AI equities, without proving a direct causal connection.
